      For 36 years of my life, as an Indian woman, my choices came from the space of making everyone around me happy. I piled myself with judgments and expectations. I believed I was dumb and wore un-intelligence as the best suit to walk around in the world. And yet people, money and luxury were always drawn towards me. So I also knew I was different.

      As a child I was exposed to the teachings of Guru Nanak, and I am deeply grateful for them. There was always some separation between my reality and those teachings, though. I couldn't bring them into my life as a whole, as if there were a spelling mistake somewhere.

      My search led me to Access Bars® and the pragmatic tools of Access Consciousness®. They brought all the jumbled pieces together. I had found something that believes in a world of creation, possibilities and kindness, like I do.

      I wasn't dumb all this while. I started to see my wrongness as my strongness.
